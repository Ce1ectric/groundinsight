    Thermal limits of earthing conductors¶

    Does an earthing conductor survive the fault current it carries? This guide checks the screens and earth wires between the stations with gi.check_conductor_limits and the earthing conductor and the electrode at each station with gi.check_node_limits, by the adiabatic short-time rating of IEC 60949 with the thermal equivalent current of IEC 60909-0. The script is thermal.py; its output is at the end of the page.

    The check¶

    For each conductor the check compares the thermal equivalent short-time current

    \[ I_{th} = I_\text{RMS}\,\sqrt{m + n} \]

    with the adiabatic limit

    \[ I_\text{adm} = \frac{k\,S}{\sqrt{t_k}}, \qquad k = K\,\sqrt{\ln\frac{\theta_f + \beta}{\theta_i + \beta}} , \]

    where \(S\) is the cross-section, \(t_k\) the short-circuit duration (t_k_s), \(m\) the DC heat factor from the peak factor \(\kappa\) (IEC 60909-0), \(n\) the AC heat factor (1 far from generators), and \(K\), \(\beta\) the material constants. utilization is \(I_{th}/I_\text{adm}\), within_limit is utilization <= 1. Every column: Result tables.

    What the check needs¶

    • On the types. A BranchType is checked when it declares conductor_material and cross_section_mm2 (optionally theta_initial_C, default 20 °C, and theta_final_C). A BusType declares its two elements separately: earthing_conductor_* for the conductor that carries the full injected current I_inj into the station, and electrode_* for the electrode that carries the share into the soil, I_bus. Mixing the two currents up is the classic sizing error — at the faulted station the earthing conductor carries the whole fault current, the electrode often a small part of it.
    • current_split (earthing_conductor_current_split, electrode_current_split, between 0 and 1, default 1) is the share one physical conductor carries when the model lumps several: 0.5 for a ring electrode reached from two sides, 1/N for N parallel legs. It is never derived — the geometry is not in the model.
    • The excitation. t_k_s and n_factor come from the fault, kappa or r_to_x from the sources that feed it (set by hand, or imported from a pandapower case — see Import from pandapower). Keyword arguments of the checks (t_k_s=, kappa=, r_to_x=, n_factor=, frequency=) override the model for a sensitivity study. Several infeeds with different R/X are combined into one current-weighted κ (SC_KAPPA_MIXED); sources without data are announced (SC_DATA_INCOMPLETE).
    • An element with half of its thermal data (a material without a cross-section) is announced (THERMAL_DATA_INCOMPLETE); one with none is reported with its currents and within_limit null — enough to size it by hand, but not a pass.
    cable_system = gi.BranchType(
    name="3x NA2XS(F)2Y 1x300RM/25 12/20 kV, trefoil",
    grounding_conductor=True,
    self_impedance_formula=(
        "(0.727/3 + pi**2*1e-4*f + j*4*pi*1e-4*f*log(658*sqrt(rho/f)/0.03151272))*l"
    ),
    mutual_impedance_formula=(
        "(pi**2*1e-4*f + j*4*pi*1e-4*f*log(658*sqrt(rho/f)/0.03151272))*l"
    ),
    conductor_material="Cu",
    cross_section_mm2=69.7,  # three 25 mm2 screens, see the data-sheet how-to
    theta_final_C=gi.final_temperature("Cu", "XLPE"),  # 250 degC, insulation limit
)
substation = gi.BusType(name="substation", system_type="Substation", voltage_level=20.0,
                        impedance_formula="0.5")
station = gi.BusType(
    name="station", system_type="Station", voltage_level=20.0,
    impedance_formula="2*rho/(3*1.13*sqrt(100.0))",
    earthing_conductor_material="Cu",               # carries the full injected current
    earthing_conductor_cross_section_mm2=50.0,
    earthing_conductor_theta_final_C=gi.final_temperature("Cu", "bare"),
    electrode_material="Steel",                     # carries the share into the soil
    electrode_cross_section_mm2=90.0,
    electrode_theta_final_C=gi.final_temperature("Steel", "galvanized"),
    electrode_current_split=0.5,                    # ring electrode, reached from two sides
)

    STATIONS = ["S1", "S2", "S3", "S4", "S5"]
FAULT_BUS = "S3"
T_K_S = 0.5
R_TO_X = 0.1
net = gi.create_network(name="feeder", frequencies=[50.0])
gi.create_bus(name="UW", type=substation, specific_earth_resistance=100.0, network=net)
for name in STATIONS:
    gi.create_bus(name=name, type=station, specific_earth_resistance=100.0, network=net)
names = ["UW"] + STATIONS
for i, (a, b) in enumerate(zip(names[:-1], names[1:], strict=True), start=1):
    gi.create_branch(name=f"K{i}", type=cable_system, from_bus=a, to_bus=b, length=1.2,
                     specific_earth_resistance=100.0, network=net)
gi.create_source(name="infeed", bus="UW", values={50.0: 1000.0}, r_to_x=R_TO_X, network=net)
gi.create_fault(name="F", bus=FAULT_BUS, t_k_s=T_K_S, network=net)
gi.run_fault(net, "F")

    conductors = gi.check_conductor_limits(net, "F")  # t_k_s, kappa, n_factor from the model
nodes = gi.check_node_limits(net, "F")

    Materials and final temperatures¶

    The tables are top-level names of the package (gi.<name>).

    IEC60949_MATERIALS holds \(K\) and \(\beta\) of IEC 60949 and a default final temperature for copper, aluminium and steel — 300 °C for all three, the value for bare buried conductors. FINAL_TEMPERATURES lists final temperatures by material and covering (bare, PVC, XLPE, EPR, and tinned copper or galvanized steel), with the source named in the module; final_temperature(material, covering) reads it and raises for a combination the table does not have instead of guessing. CABLE_INSULATION_LIMITS holds the short-circuit limits of the insulation materials (PVC 160 °C, XLPE and EPR 250 °C): the screen of an XLPE cable may not get hotter than its insulation, whatever the copper could take.

    Covering Cu Al Steel
    bare 300 °C 300 °C 300 °C
    PVC 160 °C 160 °C 160 °C
    XLPE, EPR 250 °C 250 °C 250 °C

    Check the values against the edition of EN 50522 you apply before a design decision; the tables are deliberately incomplete rather than filled with plausible numbers.

    A direct-current component¶

    The check excludes the 0 Hz component of a current: \(I_{th}\) is built from the alternating components, because \(m\) already describes the decaying DC offset of an alternating fault current. A genuine direct current that flows during \(t_k\) — a DC railway, a rectifier — adds about \(I_{dc}^2\) to \(I_{th}^2\), which the check would then miss. It reports the 0 Hz part in I_dc_A and announces it (THERMAL_DC_EXCLUDED, ModelAssumptionWarning) with the increase of \(I_{th}\) that \(I_{th}^2 = I_{ac}^2\,(m + n) + I_{dc}^2\) gives.

    dc_net = net.model_copy(deep=True)
dc_net.frequencies = [0.0, 50.0]  # re-derives every element at 0 Hz as well
dc_net.sources["infeed"].values = {0.0: 150.0, 50.0: 1000.0}
gi.run_fault(dc_net, "F")
with gi.diagnostics.collecting() as dc_records:
    with_dc = gi.check_conductor_limits(dc_net, "F")

K2: I_th = 870.9 A of I_adm = 17874.6 A, utilization 0.049

K5: I_th = 54.6 A of I_adm = 17874.6 A, utilization 0.003
kappa = 1.7460, m = 0.06825, k = 181.3 A*s^0.5/mm2
S3 earthing_conductor: I = 1000.0 A (split 1), I_adm = 13765.9 A, within limit: True
S3 electrode: I = 32.6 A (split 0.5), I_adm = 8967.6 A, within limit: True
not assessed (no thermal data): ['UW']
with 150 A DC, K1: I_dc = 106.6 A, I_ac = 836.1 A, I_th = 864.1 A; ['THERMAL_DC_EXCLUDED']

    Check it by hand. With R/X = 0.1, \(\kappa = 1.02 + 0.98\,e^{-0.3} = 1.7460\); at 50 Hz and \(t_k = 0.5\) s, \(m = \frac{1}{2 f t_k \ln(\kappa - 1)}\left(e^{4 f t_k \ln(\kappa - 1)} - 1\right) = 0.06825\). Copper from 20 °C to 250 °C gives \(k = 226\,\sqrt{\ln\frac{250 + 234.5}{20 + 234.5}} = 181.3\ \text{A}\sqrt{\text{s}}/\text{mm}^2\), so the screens of 69.7 mm² admit \(181.3 \cdot 69.7/\sqrt{0.5} = 17\,875\) A. The earthing conductor at the faulted station S3 carries the full 1000 A, its electrode \(2 \cdot 32.6 = 65.2\) A, of which one side of the ring takes half. The test suite recomputes \(k\), \(I_\text{adm}\), \(I_{th}\) and the node currents this way.
